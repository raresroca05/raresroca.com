01 My story

From WordPress shops to platforms millions use.

I went to school in Cluj-Napoca and I still live here. I started by building online shops, fell for Ruby, and have spent most of my career in remote product teams across Europe and the US.

  1. 2015

    First paid work

    Freelance WordPress developer · Resetar M, then Casa din Piatra

    WordPress themes and plugins for e-commerce sites. Fast pages, phones first, and clients who notice every detail.

  2. 2018

    Ruby on Rails, in Cluj

    Software engineer · Greppy Systems, then Wolfpack Digital

    Rails backends and APIs for client apps, working side by side with design and mobile teams. This is where Rails became home.

  3. 2019

    Bigger systems, and going independent

    Software engineer · 3Pillar Global

    Backend services and SQL tuning for customer products. The same year I started taking remote work on my own, and started my degree at Babeș-Bolyai University.

  4. 2020

    Moving 15 million users to Rails

    Senior full-stack engineer · THENX & Heria Pro

    I led the move of a legacy PHP API to Ruby on Rails for a fitness platform with more than 15 million users. I moved the data from MySQL to PostgreSQL and fixed duplicate users and Stripe subscriptions on the way, without breaking anything for paying customers.

  5. 2021

    Commerce at scale

    Senior full-stack engineer · Mayzing, formerly Moteefe

    Rails services for a print-on-demand commerce platform. Faster queries, safer releases and better monitoring, so incidents got shorter.

  6. 2022

    Food delivery, where every second counts

    Senior full-stack engineer · Deliveroo

    Backend and frontend work in a high-scale ordering system. Refactored old services, tuned the database and tightened CI so releases stopped being scary.

  7. 2023

    Healthcare, and wearing every hat

    Senior full-stack engineer · Doctolib

    Rails and React features for a healthcare platform millions of patients rely on. Every two weeks I rotated through product manager, feature architect and tech lead roles.

  8. 2025

    Customer service for 30,000+ businesses

    Senior full-stack engineer · Intercom

    Product features end to end in Rails and React, for the customer service platform behind Fin, Intercom's AI agent. A large, mature codebase taught me to ship small, ship often and own what I ship.

  9. 2025

    AI becomes part of the job

    Personal project · Assista AI

    In parallel I built a Rails AI assistant connected to OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Linear. It changed how I work: today I plan, build and review with Claude Code and my own custom skills, with tests as the final judge.

  10. Now

    Health apps for rare diseases

    Senior full-stack engineer · Florio · since March 2026

    Florio makes apps for people living with haemophilia, ITP, PNH and other rare conditions, and for their care teams. I build features on the Rails platform those apps share, in small, careful steps, because here every detail is someone's health.

02 What I do

I build it, ship it and own it.

A

Rails backends and APIs

APIs for web and mobile apps, clean data models, and code the next person can read without a guide. My home ground.

B

Jobs that don't get lost

Sidekiq and Redis workflows with retries, queues, webhooks and third-party syncs that recover on their own.

C

Data you can trust

PostgreSQL and MySQL migrations, clean-ups, indexes and query tuning. I fix the cause, not the symptom.

D

Owning it in production

I don't hand over and walk away. Logs, SQL, the fix, the test, the rollout and the check that it really worked.

E

React front ends

React and TypeScript screens that fit the backend behind them, built with the product team, not after it.

F

AI in the workflow

Claude Code, Cursor and custom skills to explore, build and review faster. Docs, code and tests stay the source of truth.

How I work

  • Own the outcome, not the ticket. From the first question to the follow-up after release, it's mine.
  • Small steps beat big rewrites. I modernise old code bit by bit, with tests and monitoring around what matters.
  • Talk in impact. I explain the risk, the options and my recommendation, in words everyone in the room understands.
  • Ask early. Unclear requirements get questions on day one, and a small first version if doubt remains.
